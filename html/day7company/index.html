Chennai Companies

Chennai is a major IT hub in South India, home to many global and domestic tech companies.

Chennai is one of India's oldest and most established IT hubs. It is home to global tech giants like TCS, Infosys, and Zoho, along with innovative product companies like Freshworks. The city's strong engineering talent pool and affordable infrastructure make it a preferred destination for IT and BPO services. Chennai's IT corridor along OMR (Old Mahabalipuram Road) is packed with tech parks and startup incubators, making it a vibrant ecosystem for both domestic and international companies.

  2. Freshworks
  3. TCS
  4. Infosys

Bangalore Companies

Bangalore, known as the Silicon Valley of India, hosts the world's top tech giants.

Bangalore, often called the "Silicon Valley of India," is the country's most dynamic IT hub. It hosts the Indian headquarters of global tech leaders like Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and Flipkart. The city is known for its startup culture, world-class infrastructure, and high concentration of tech talent. Bangalore's pleasant climate and cosmopolitan lifestyle attract professionals from all over India and the world, making it a melting pot of innovation and technology.

  • Google
  • Microsoft
  • Amazon
  • Flipkart

Hyderabad Companies

Hyderabad is a rapidly growing IT destination with many global companies setting up their offices.

Hyderabad has emerged as a major IT destination, often referred to as "Cyberabad." The city is home to the Indian offices of Microsoft, Google, Deloitte, and Tech Mahindra. With its state-of-the-art HITEC City and Gachibowli IT hubs, Hyderabad offers excellent infrastructure, a skilled workforce, and a lower cost of living compared to other metros. The city's proactive government policies and focus on innovation have made it a top choice for global tech companies setting up operations in India

  • Microsoft
  • Google
  • Deloitte
  • Tech Mahindra
