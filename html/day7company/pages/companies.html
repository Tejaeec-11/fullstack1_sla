List of All IT Companies

Here is a complete list of top IT companies across India.

Chennai

  • ZOHO

    Zoho is a Chennai-based global software company known for its cloud-based business applications. It offers a suite of products like Zoho CRM, Zoho Books, and Zoho Mail, catering to millions of users worldwide. Zoho is privately held and is one of the few Indian companies to compete with global giants like Salesforce and Microsoft. The company is also known for its focus on employee welfare and its strong presence in the rural tech sector.

  • Freshworks

    Freshworks is a Chennai-born SaaS company that provides customer engagement software. Its flagship products include Freshdesk, Freshsales, and Freshservice. Freshworks has a global presence with offices in the US, UK, Australia, and Germany. It is one of the first Indian SaaS companies to be listed on NASDAQ. The company is known for its innovative culture, user-friendly products, and strong customer support.

  • TCS

    TCS is India's largest IT services company and a part of the Tata Group. Headquartered in Mumbai, it has a massive presence in Chennai, with multiple delivery centres across the city. TCS offers a wide range of services including software development, consulting, and business process outsourcing. It is one of the most valued IT companies in the world and serves clients across banking, healthcare, retail, and manufacturing sectors.

  • Infosys

    Infosys is a global leader in consulting, technology, and outsourcing services. It has a significant presence in Chennai, with one of its largest development centres located in the city. The company is known for its strong focus on innovation, digital transformation, and sustainability. Infosys was founded in 1981 and is headquartered in Bangalore, but its Chennai campus is a major hub for its delivery operations.

Bangalore

  • Google

    Google's Bangalore office is one of its largest engineering hubs outside the US. The campus, located in the heart of the city, focuses on product development, cloud services, and AI research. Google Bangalore plays a key role in developing products like Google Pay, Search, and Maps for the Indian market. The company is also known for its vibrant work culture, open spaces, and employee-friendly policies.

  • Microsoft

    Microsoft's Bangalore campus is a major research and development centre, often referred to as the "Microsoft India Development Centre." It is one of the company's largest facilities outside the US, working on cloud computing, AI, and enterprise products like Azure and Office 365. The campus is known for its innovative work environment, diverse talent pool, and focus on inclusive technology.

  • Amazon

    Amazon's Bangalore office is a key technology hub for the company's operations in India. It focuses on e-commerce platforms, cloud computing (AWS), and data analytics. The campus is involved in developing customer-facing applications and seller tools. Amazon Bangalore is also known for its fast-paced work culture and strong emphasis on innovation and customer obsession.

  • Flipkart

    Flipkart, headquartered in Bangalore, is India's largest homegrown e-commerce platform. The company was founded in 2007 and has revolutionised online shopping in India. Its Bangalore campus is the nerve centre for product development, logistics, and customer service. Flipkart is known for its startup culture, dynamic work environment, and focus on serving the Indian consumer with localised solutions.

Hyderabad

  • Microsoft

    Microsoft's Hyderabad campus is one of the company's largest development centres in India. It plays a key role in developing enterprise products like Azure, Office 365, and Dynamics. The campus is known for its world-class infrastructure, green spaces, and focus on research and innovation. It is a major employer in Hyderabad's HITEC City, attracting top engineering talent from across the country.

  • Google

    Google's Hyderabad office is a key centre for its cloud and AI initiatives. The campus is located in the Gachibowli IT hub and focuses on cloud infrastructure, enterprise solutions, and product development. Google Hyderabad works closely with its Bangalore office on several projects. The company is known for its inclusive culture, employee benefits, and commitment to diversity and innovation.

  • Deloitte

    Deloitte's Hyderabad office is a significant hub for its consulting and advisory services. It provides services in areas like audit, taxation, risk management, and digital transformation. Deloitte Hyderabad is one of the firm's largest delivery centres in India, serving clients across the US, Europe, and Asia. The company is known for its professional work environment, learning opportunities, and focus on client success.

  • Tech Mahindra

    Tech Mahindra's Hyderabad centre is a key delivery hub for its IT and business process outsourcing services. The company is part of the Mahindra Group and offers services in telecom, healthcare, and banking sectors. Its Hyderabad campus is known for its modern infrastructure, employee-friendly policies, and focus on digital transformation. Tech Mahindra is also a leader in 5G and AI-driven solutions.
